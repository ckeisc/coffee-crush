On the counter — the treats

✦ Espresso Blast: born from a match of 4. Swap it or match it to fire a full row + column clear.
★ Golden Bean Bomb: born from a match of 5. Swap it with any treat to clear every treat of that kind on the board.

🧾 Orders: the trays above the board are customer orders, arriving in a set sequence. Each match serves one of its treats into a tray that needs it — clear three beans, serve one bean. Finish a tray and it stamps itself ✓ Shipped!, flies out with a bonus, and the next order slides in — serve every order in the level's sequence before your moves run out to pass the level.
⏱ Expiry (Level 2+): Level 1 orders have no timer — take your time. From Level 2 up, the timer in a tray's lower-right corner counts down to when that order expires — it turns red for the last 10 seconds. An expired order leaves the counter, the next order takes its place, and the expired one comes back later in the sequence.

How to play

Welcome to the café! Swap neighbouring treats to line up 3 or more of a kind. Serve every customer order on the trays above the board before your moves run out — clearing all the level's orders is what passes the level.

  • Swipe a treat toward a neighbour to swap them — one swipe does it, no need to tap first.
  • Orders arrive in a set sequence. Each match serves one of its treats into a tray that needs it; finish every tray to clear the level. Level 1 has no order timer; from Level 2 up, the ⏱ timer at a tray's lower right shows when it expires — expired orders leave and come back later in the sequence.
  • Match 4 brews an Espresso Blast ✦ — fire it to clear its whole row and column.
  • Match 5 brews a Golden Bean Bomb ★ — swap it with any treat to clear all treats of that kind.
  • Chains pay double, triple… each cascade in one move multiplies your points and pops higher.
  • No swap available? The board reshuffles itself, free of charge.
